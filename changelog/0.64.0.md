# 0.64.0

### Features

* Add `arc(p, r, a, b)` to `@pattern` and `@scene`.
* Add `rand(p, k)`, and allow `fill` inside `repeat`.
* Read `shape(d)` as the inside of `d = 0`.
* Let a texture read its own variables with `$name`.
* Allow `//` comments in `@shape`, `@plot` and `@tile`.
* Allow a trailing `...` in `@p`, `@pd` and `@pnr`.
* Apply a pseudo after a cell selector, and match a comma list of them.
* Read math in the shape commands, and `deg` and `turn` in a math call.
* Keep a joined `g`'s curves as arcs, and grow its outline with `grow`.

### Fixes

* Keep `evenodd` and a repeated corner in a `g|op`.
* Make `gcd` non-negative.
* Draw no more `@scene` copies than the count.
* Read `@R.t` once inside `@plot`.
* Keep `π` after a name, and a comment in a quoted selector.
* Shorten every warning and error to three words.
