# 0.70.1

This is a small cleanup release.

### Breaking changes

* Rename `@flipH` and `@flipV` to `@flip.x` and `@flip.y`.
* Remove the `@multiple` and `@ms` aliases of `@m` and `@M`.
* Drop the gradient palettes of `@palette`, so the seeds that drew one draw other colors.

### Features

* Place a cell at the center of a `@tile` piece with `@place: @tile.circle(…)`.
* Read `rand()` in `$()` as `random()`.

### Fixes

* List `color` in the needs of `prerender()` and `image()` when `@palette` leaves out its base color, instead of warning.
* Shorten the warnings.
